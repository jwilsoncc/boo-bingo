Trainer Answer Key

Hidden by default so players on the projector cannot see the answers. Open it after you reveal the hold reason and have asked the room: why is this a hold, and what are your next steps?

All scenarios are fictional. No real member names or identifying information are used. Every timeframe, form name, and system step must follow your company procedures; where this key says "confirm with company policy," get the answer from your supervisor or compliance lead before training.
